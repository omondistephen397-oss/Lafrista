The story behind Lafrista

Thoughtful fashion, made personal.

Founded by Fridah Anyango Omondi, Lafrista brings together a love of personal style, considered fabrics and thoughtful tailoring.

Every design starts with you: what you want to wear, how you want it to fit and how you want to feel.
